How it works

From your gift to a growing forest.

No middle layers, no guesswork — just trees in the ground and proof they're thriving.

1

You give

Plant trees once or monthly. 100% of your gift funds planting and forest protection on the ground.

2

We plant & protect

Local teams plant native species and guard standing forest, paid fairly for work that lasts.

3

You track the impact

Watch your trees on the live map and get satellite-verified updates on growth and carbon.

Our mission

A cooler planet, rebuilt one root at a time.

Rooted restores degraded land, defends the forests still standing, and helps communities thrive alongside nature. We measure everything — because the climate doesn't run on good intentions.
