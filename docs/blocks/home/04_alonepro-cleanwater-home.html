Our mission

Clean water changes everything.

When a village gets clean water, girls go back to school, mothers stay healthy, and whole communities lift themselves out of poverty. We make that water permanent — owned and maintained by the people who depend on it.

  • Every well is community-owned from day one
  • Local mechanics trained for repairs that last
  • Live monitoring — we know which wells are flowing
