Response protocol

The first 72 hours decide everything.

T + 0H

Alert

Within minutes of an event, our ops centre verifies need and puts the nearest teams on standby.

T + 6H

Deploy

Pre-positioned kits and first responders move toward the impact zone by air, road or boat.

T + 24H

Relieve

Clean water, emergency shelter and medical care reach survivors on the ground.

T + 72H

Recover

We shift from rescue to rebuilding — and stay until the community can carry on.
