FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What services does Medikale provide?
Free clinics, mobile health units, diagnostics, maternal and child care, mental health support and emergency response — all at no cost to the patient.
What safety and hygiene measures are followed?
We follow strict clinical hygiene protocols, sterilise all equipment, and our teams are trained and certified to national standards.
Can a family member stay with the patient?
Yes — we encourage a family member or carer to be present, and we have comfortable spaces for them throughout care.
How can I give feedback or make a complaint?
You can speak to any team member, call our patient line, or email care@medikale.org. Every piece of feedback is reviewed.
Do you accept health insurance?
No insurance is ever needed — Medikale is free at the point of care, funded entirely by donors and partners.
