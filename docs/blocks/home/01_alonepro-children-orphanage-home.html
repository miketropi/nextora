Our mission

A safe home, a warm meal, and a future worth dreaming about.

Haven runs family-style children's homes where kids who've lost their parents grow up loved, safe and in school. We keep siblings together, invest in each child's education, and stay with them all the way into adulthood.

  • Family-style care — never institutional dormitories
  • Full schooling, healthcare and counselling for every child
  • Transparent reporting — you see exactly where your gift goes
