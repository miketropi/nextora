Who we are

We don't fix people. We fix the barriers.

Disability isn't the problem — inaccessible places, jobs and attitudes are. Open Doors works to remove those barriers, so people of every ability can live, work and belong on their own terms.

  • Designed with disabled people, never just for them
  • Support across autism, mobility, vision, hearing & more
  • Advocacy that pushes for access as the standard, not a favour
