Why it matters

Good food, to the people who need it — today.

Every year, tonnes of perfectly good food go to waste while millions go hungry. We bridge that gap: rescuing surplus from farms, shops and kitchens, and getting it to families, shelters and schools the same day. No judgement, just food.
