Our mission

Rescue. Rehabilitate. Rehome. Repeat.

Refuge is a no-kill shelter and wildlife sanctuary. We take in the injured, the abandoned and the overlooked — give them medical care, time and trust — and find them the home they were always meant to have.

  • A strict no-kill promise — every healthy animal gets a home
  • On-site vets, rehab and a dedicated wildlife wing
  • Spay, neuter and release programs to break the cycle
